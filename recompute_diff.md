### DEFINITION_MISMATCH

| # | item | detail |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | the static conformal shift | Equation (7) defines `qV_stat = Q_{1-alpha}({S_t})`, "the empirical (1-alpha)-quantile of the calibration scores". The pipeline computes the finite-sample split-conformal order statistic, the `ceil((n+1)(1-alpha))`-th of the sorted scores. Using the manuscript's definition, my recomputation missed every published `qV`; using the code's, it reproduced them to 1e-16. The two differ materially — on DJCI the plain quantile is **-0.000176** and the order statistic is **+0.000708**, opposite signs. The order-statistic convention is stated in the paper only for the *rolling* estimator, in the Data and Code section. |
