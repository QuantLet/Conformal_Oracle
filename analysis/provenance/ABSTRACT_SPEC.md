# What the abstract must claim, written before it is written

Phase 4, 2026-08-29. The body and its numbering are closed, so this is written
once against the final structure.

## The three claims, in the fixed order

1. **Proposition 5.1 with Corollary 5.2, and Proposition 5.3.** The Basel
   classification identifies $\mathrm{sign}(\pi - \tau)$ in the limit and nothing
   else; the interval it cannot resolve does not shrink with evidence; and on the
   panel a zone upgrade is a **strict sub-event** of a move toward nominal.
2. **The conformal guarantee, with what it does not give.** Marginal coverage
   exactly in finite samples under exchangeability and asymptotically under
   geometric $\beta$-mixing with an explicit remainder; the separation condition
   the contiguous split does not meet, reported rather than assumed; and the
   indifference to the forecaster, which is why the corrected column cannot rank.
3. **The indication result**, which follows from the first two.

Non-identification is **not** a claim here. At most one motivating clause, and
only if the word count allows.

## The one empirical figure, if only one fits

Proposition 5.3 measured: **0** of the zone upgrades occur without a move toward
nominal, and **70** pairs move closer with no zone change. That is the theorem
measured on data, anyone holding the table can check it, and no precedent for it
was found in the K0b reading. It earns words ahead of $\delta^\star$, which is a
supporting quantity and appears in Section 8.

## What the abstract must not do

- **Do not price the cost in zones.** The 20 zone upgrades the deployable rule
  gives up decompose into **9** real losses and **11** pairs the correction would
  have made worse on the quantile score. An abstract that says the rule "costs
  20" contradicts, two sentences later, the claim it opened with --- that a zone
  is a coarser reading of the same movement. The cost is stated in the score,
  with the zone figure named as what the traffic light counts.
- **Do not claim priority** over the weakness of exceedance-based backtests.
- **No editorial "we" as subject.** The current abstract opens "We show that",
  twice; the register reference measures Related Literature and this class of
  passage at zero.
- **No new literal.** Every figure is an existing macro.

## Mechanical constraints

Under **250 words** for the abstract proper. **Five** keywords; "Reproducibility"
is already absent and stays absent. The title already reads *An Intervention with
an Indication* and promises no audit, so it is left alone.

## How this will be checked

Word count computed after macro expansion is not the right count --- a reviewer
reads the rendered text, so the count is taken on the expanded text with each
macro standing for the word or number it prints. Guard 2 and check 5 run over
the result like any other prose.
