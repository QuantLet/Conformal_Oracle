# Register reference

Step 0. Written before any edit to the manuscript. Every register change is
judged against this document.

The question is not whether R3 argues better than the IJF version. It is **who is
speaking**. Three matched pairs, then the measurement.

---

## Pair 1 — Data (IJF §3.3.1 / R3 §3.3.1)

**The two are byte-identical**, apart from one pointer changed from
`Appendix~\ref{app:assets}` to `Supplement~S.7`.

That is the most useful single observation in this document. The register has not
drifted uniformly. It has drifted **exactly where the audit rewrote the text**,
and the sections the audit never touched still speak in the original voice. The
target register is therefore not a reconstruction — it is present in the same
file, in the passages nobody had to defend.

## Pair 2 — Static recalibration (IJF / R3 §8.1)

**IJF.** The narrator is invisible. Three consecutive sentences of the form
*"Among the 144 model–asset pairs …, 127 achieve …"*, then one interpretive
sentence: *"Green Zone recovery in the replacement regime should therefore be
interpreted as restored unconditional coverage, not as full conditional-coverage
adequacy."* One instruction to the reader, delivered impersonally, at the end.

**R3.** The same facts, and four additional sentences in which the narrator is
the subject:

| R3 sentence | what it does |
|---|---|
| "What the ordering by R̄ adds is the *amount* of correction required to get there." | tells the reader what the table is for |
| "**We deliberately do not partition the panel.**" | announces an editorial decision, as a paragraph opener |
| "Any threshold would inherit that arbitrariness, and the continuous statistic conveys the same information without it." | defends the decision |
| "**We report that subset because** counting a degenerate table as a pass, as is common, would turn the same evidence into a 78.4% pass rate …" | explains why the reported quantity was chosen |
| "The degeneracy rate is itself a result, and Section 6 treats it as one" | instructs the reader how to read |

The facts are identical. What has been added is a narrator standing beside the
table, explaining that he has been careful. Note that the *content* of the fourth
row is worth keeping — counting a degenerate table as a pass would give 78.4% —
but it can be said without "we report that subset because".

## Pair 3 — Limitations (IJF §8.1 / R3 §9.1)

Near-identical, and R3 is marginally tighter. IJF: *"this is a structural
limitation, not a tuning issue"*. R3 drops that clause.

This pair matters because it calibrates the target. The IJF voice is **not free
of definition-by-negation** — it uses it, at low density. The instruction is
therefore to return to the IJF *rate*, not to zero. A paper with no negations
would be a different distortion.

---

## Measurement

Whole-document counts, normalised per 10,000 words of body text.

| pattern | IJF | R3 | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **narrator comments on his own choice** | **0.8** | **17.4** | **21×** |
| definition by negation | 2.4 | 10.0 | 4.2× |
| editorial "we" as sentence subject | 4.9 | 11.2 | 2.3× |
| second-person imperative | 0.0 | 3.1 | from zero |

The first row is the finding. It is not a stylistic preference operating at the
margin; it is a twenty-one-fold change in how often the narrator turns from the
data to the reader to characterise his own conduct.

**The aphorism pattern is not captured by any count I could construct.** Short
declaratives are no denser in R3 than in IJF by word length (63 against 52 per
10k) or by position in the paragraph (48 instances in each). What has changed is
*which* sentences take that form and where they land — a closing flourish after a
result rather than a pointer to an appendix. That cannot be measured, so it is
governed by the named list and the three-place budget, not by a metric.

holds every second-person imperative in the manuscript.

## The test each edit must pass

1. **Does the fact survive?** The 0.147 margin stays printed. The 21.3% residual
   stays. The five unsourced claims stay. Only the sentence explaining why they
   are printed goes.
2. **Is the subject the data or the paper?** "The band edge was chosen by hand"
   reports a fact about the method. "We report this rather than adjusting the
   edge" reports a fact about the authors.
3. **Would the IJF version have written this sentence?** Pair 1 shows the IJF
   voice is available in the same file for comparison.

## What this document does not license

Hedging, softening a limitation, neutralising a negative result, or deleting an
uncomfortable figure. The measurement above runs in one direction only: R3 talks
about itself twenty-one times too often. Talking about itself less is not the
same as claiming less, and any edit that reduces what the paper claims has failed
the first test above.
