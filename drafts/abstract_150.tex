A Value-at-Risk backtest reports \emph{that} a forecast series miscovers, not
\emph{why}. We show that the exceedance sequence identifies exactly one
functional of the joint law of forecast and return, so every test measurable with
respect to it --- Kupiec, Christoffersen, the Basel traffic light, the dynamic
quantile test --- has power equal to its size against alternatives agreeing on
it. One number quantifies the gap. Under a unimodal return law a Value-at-Risk
understated by \nGapUndUni\% is exactly unidentifiable, holding coverage,
exceedance clustering, point-forecast accuracy and predictive dispersion fixed;
structural checks on the series reduce this to \nGapUndGateNow\% and do not close
it. The same quantity explains the panel: across \nMainAssets{} assets and
\nMainForecasters{} forecasters, a sampling default in a public Chronos-T5
checkpoint drives the 1\% violation rate to \nPiSmallDefaultOne{} against
\nPiSmallAnalyticOne{} read in closed form --- outside the blind region, which is
why coverage rejects it everywhere while rejecting \nSeqKupiecRejRawOne\% of the
panel besides. Detection is not diagnosis.
