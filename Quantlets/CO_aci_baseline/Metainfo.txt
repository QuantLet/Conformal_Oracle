Name of Quantlet: CO_aci_baseline

Published in: Recalibrating Tail Event Forecasts under Temporal Dependence

Description: Adaptive Conformal Inference baseline. Runs ACI (Gibbs and Candes 2021) with step size gamma selected from {0.001, 0.005, 0.01, 0.05} by first-half validation over all 216 model-asset pairs from cached forecasts, and compares it head-to-head with the static and 250-day rolling conformal estimators on the test split. Reports violation rate, Kupiec and Christoffersen p-values, Basel traffic-light zone shares, mean |VaR| (capital proxy), and a VaR-path volatility measure (sd of day-over-day corrected VaR changes) capturing operational smoothness. Emits a data-driven ACI-vs-rolling verdict and a gamma-sensitivity table. Uses conformal_oracle.recalibration.ACICalibrator.

Keywords: conformal prediction, VaR, adaptive conformal inference, ACI, backtesting, Kupiec, Christoffersen, Basel traffic light, capital

See also: CO_baseline_comparison, CO_rolling_qV
