Name of Quantlet: CO_diagnostic_scale

Published in: Recalibrating Tail Event Forecasts under Temporal Dependence

Description: Empirical two-parameter scale diagnostic. For each of the 216 model-asset pairs (9 forecasters x 24 assets) it fits the location-scale map VaR_cp = a_hat + b_hat * VaR_raw by alpha-level linear quantile regression on the calibration split, using conformal_oracle.recalibration.diagnose_scale, and reports per forecaster the share of the one-parameter conformal shift attributable to the multiplicative (scale) term. Diagnostic only: the k=1 conformal design from the alpha*T constraint is unchanged; the map is not proposed as a competing corrector.

Keywords: conformal prediction, VaR, quantile regression, scale correction, diagnostic, location-scale, tail risk

See also: CO_identification, CO_baseline_comparison
