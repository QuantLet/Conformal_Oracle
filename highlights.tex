\noindent\textit{Recalibrating Tail Risk Forecasts under Temporal Dependence}

\bigskip

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=8pt]

\item A one-parameter conformal shift recalibrates extreme-quantile forecasts and doubles as a signed audit statistic for tail information content.

\item The correction magnitude separates forecasters into two regimes differing by an order of magnitude: signal-preserving, where the shift adjusts an informative base, and replacement, where the shift is the forecast.

\item Rolling recalibration substantially improves extreme-tail coverage and outperforms richer alternatives, consistent with the prediction that additional parameters add variance faster than they reduce bias at the 1\% tail.

\item A within-family contrast between sample-based and quantile-grid foundation-model outputs points to the predictive interface as a major contributor to tail miscalibration.

\item Scalar recalibration restores marginal coverage but does not restore conditional violation independence, identifying a clear boundary for low-dimensional post-hoc correction.

\end{itemize}
